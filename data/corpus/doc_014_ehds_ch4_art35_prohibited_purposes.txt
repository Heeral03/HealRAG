EHDS Chapter IV Article 35 - Prohibited secondary uses of health data: The following secondary uses of electronic health data shall be prohibited: (a) taking decisions detrimental to a natural person based on their electronic health data, including increasing insurance premiums or refusing insurance contracts; (b) advertising or marketing activities directed at health professionals or natural persons; (c) providing access to data to third parties not authorized under a data permit; (d) developing products or services that may harm public health or undermine safety.

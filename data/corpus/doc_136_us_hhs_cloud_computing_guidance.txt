HHS OCR guidance on HIPAA and cloud computing: A cloud service provider (CSP) that creates, receives, maintains or transmits ePHI on behalf of a covered entity or business associate is a business associate, and a BAA is required. This applies even if the CSP stores only encrypted ePHI and does not hold the decryption key, because the CSP still maintains the ePHI. The 'conduit exception' applies only to transmission-only services that do not store PHI other than transiently. A covered entity may use a CSP to store ePHI outside the United States; HIPAA does not prohibit it, but the risk analysis should account for the additional risks. A BAA with a CSP should address which security responsibilities fall to the CSP and which to the customer, and the CSP must report security incidents and breaches. Using a CSP that does not sign a BAA with a covered entity violates HIPAA. Mobile device and app use is covered by related OCR guidance.

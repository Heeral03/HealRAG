Clinical terminologies used with FHIR and national health data models: SNOMED CT is a comprehensive multilingual clinical terminology of concepts, descriptions and relationships, used for problems, findings, procedures and clinical documentation; it is mandated in NHS England and is a required terminology in the US for problems and procedures under USCDI. LOINC (Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes) identifies laboratory tests and clinical observations such as vital signs, for example LOINC 8867-4 for heart rate and 29463-7 for body weight. ICD-10 is the WHO classification of diseases used for diagnosis coding and statistics; the US uses ICD-10-CM for diagnoses and ICD-10-PCS for inpatient procedures, and the UK uses ICD-10 with the OPCS-4 procedure classification. RxNorm, maintained by the US National Library of Medicine, normalises clinical drug names for US interoperability; the UK equivalent is the NHS Dictionary of Medicines and Devices (dm+d). FHIR resources bind coded elements to these code systems using CodeableConcept, system URIs and value sets, which supports semantic interoperability between UK Core, US Core and the European EHDS exchange format.

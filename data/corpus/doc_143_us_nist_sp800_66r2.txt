NIST Special Publication 800-66 Revision 2, 'Implementing the HIPAA Security Rule: A Cybersecurity Resource' (February 2024): Guides regulated entities in applying the HIPAA Security Rule using NIST's risk management methods. It maps each Security Rule standard and implementation specification to NIST Cybersecurity Framework subcategories, to SP 800-53 security controls and to SP 800-171 requirements, and gives sample questions for assessing compliance. It follows the SP 800-30 risk assessment steps: prepare for the assessment, identify the scope and where ePHI is created, received, maintained or transmitted, identify threats and vulnerabilities, assess current controls, determine likelihood and impact, and determine risk levels. The publication uses the NIST Risk Management Framework to select, implement and monitor controls, and recommends documenting results. It is voluntary guidance, not a legal requirement, but its use is evidence of recognised security practices.

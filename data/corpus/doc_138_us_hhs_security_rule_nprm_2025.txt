HHS proposed update to the HIPAA Security Rule, published 6 January 2025 as a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (not a final rule): HHS proposed to strengthen the cybersecurity of ePHI by removing the distinction between required and addressable implementation specifications, so that almost all would be required. Proposed new requirements include: a written technology asset inventory and network map reviewed at least every 12 months; a more specific risk analysis; multi-factor authentication for most access; encryption of ePHI at rest and in transit, with limited exceptions; vulnerability scanning at least every six months and penetration testing at least every 12 months; network segmentation; a requirement to restore critical systems within 72 hours of a loss; written incident response plans; annual compliance audits; and written verification from business associates, at least annually, that they have deployed required technical safeguards. Because it is only a proposed rule, the current Security Rule in 45 CFR 164.302-164.318 remains in force until HHS issues a final rule. Readers should check the Federal Register for the current status.

UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, which received Royal Assent on 19 June 2025: Amends the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, with provisions being brought into force in stages by regulations. Key changes for health data: a statutory definition of scientific research that covers research that could reasonably be described as scientific, whether publicly or privately funded, and a broader ability to obtain consent for an area of scientific research; a new lawful basis of recognised legitimate interests for specified purposes without a balancing test; replacement of UK GDPR Art. 22 with new Articles 22A to 22D, which permit more automated decision-making, with safeguards, but restrict solely automated decisions based on special category data to certain conditions; a controller's duty to take reasonable and proportionate steps in response to subject access requests; and a statutory complaints process that requires data subjects to complain to the controller first. It converts the Information Commissioner's Office into a corporate body, the Information Commission, increases PECR fines to UK GDPR levels, and establishes digital verification services and smart data schemes. The Act also provides for common information standards for health and adult social care IT.

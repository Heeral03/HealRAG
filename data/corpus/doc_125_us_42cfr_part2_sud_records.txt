42 CFR Part 2: Federal rules, issued under 42 USC 290dd-2, that protect the confidentiality of records of patients treated by federally assisted substance use disorder (SUD) programmes. Part 2 is stricter than HIPAA: SUD treatment records generally may not be disclosed, or used in civil, criminal, administrative or legislative proceedings against the patient, without the patient's written consent or a specific court order meeting Part 2 criteria. The 2024 Final Rule, implementing the CARES Act of 2020, aligns Part 2 more closely with HIPAA: a patient may give a single consent for all future uses and disclosures for treatment, payment and health care operations; HIPAA covered entities and business associates that receive Part 2 records under that consent may redisclose them as HIPAA permits; breach notification now applies to Part 2 records; and the rule adopts HIPAA-style civil and criminal penalties. The rule took effect on 16 April 2024 and entities had to comply by 16 February 2026. Patients also gain rights to an accounting of disclosures and to request restrictions, and new protections cover use of the records in legal proceedings.

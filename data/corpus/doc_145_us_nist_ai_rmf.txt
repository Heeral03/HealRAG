NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), released 26 January 2023: A voluntary framework for managing risks of AI systems to individuals, organisations and society. Its core has four functions: Govern (a cross-cutting culture of risk management, accountability and policies), Map (establish context and identify risks), Measure (analyse, assess and track risks with quantitative and qualitative methods) and Manage (prioritise and act on risks). Characteristics of trustworthy AI in the framework are: valid and reliable; safe; secure and resilient; accountable and transparent; explainable and interpretable; privacy-enhanced; and fair with harmful bias managed. In July 2024 NIST published the Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1), which lists risks specific to generative AI, including confabulation (hallucination), data privacy, information integrity, and harmful bias, and suggested actions. In US healthcare, the AI RMF supports compliance with FDA expectations for AI-enabled devices and ONC's decision support intervention transparency requirements, and parallels the EU AI Act's risk-based approach.

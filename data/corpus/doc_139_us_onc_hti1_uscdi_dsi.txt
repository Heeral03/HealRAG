ONC Health Data, Technology, and Interoperability (HTI-1) Final Rule, published 9 January 2024, effective 8 February 2024: Updates the ONC Health IT Certification Program. It adopts USCDI version 3 as the baseline standard for certified health IT, replacing USCDI v1, with a compliance date of 1 January 2026. It replaces the older Clinical Decision Support criterion with a decision support interventions (DSI) criterion that requires certified health IT developers to give users transparency about predictive models, including algorithmic AI: source attributes such as the intended use, training data and cautions on bias and fairness (31 source attributes for predictive DSIs), plus intervention risk management practices for validity, reliability, safety, security and privacy. It also requires FHIR-based API capabilities aligned with the HL7 FHIR US Core Implementation Guide and SMART App Launch, and adds a Patient Demographics and Observations criterion. HTI-1 also updated information blocking exceptions.

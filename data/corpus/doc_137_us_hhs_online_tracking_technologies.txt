HHS OCR bulletin on online tracking technologies: Issued December 2022 and updated 18 March 2024, it explains that HIPAA covered entities and business associates that use tracking technologies such as cookies, web beacons, pixels and session-replay scripts on websites and mobile apps must comply with the Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules when the technology collects PHI. OCR's position was that on authenticated pages such as patient portals the tracking data is PHI, and that disclosing it to a vendor such as an analytics or advertising company requires a HIPAA-compliant authorization or a BAA with that vendor, and that an impermissible disclosure is a breach. In June 2024, in American Hospital Association v. Becerra, a federal court in the Northern District of Texas vacated the portion of the bulletin addressing the 'proscribed combination' of an IP address with a visit to an unauthenticated public webpage on a health topic. The rest of the bulletin, including its treatment of authenticated pages and the BAA requirement, remains. Separately, the FTC and state laws regulate tracking technologies used by organisations outside HIPAA.

UK Network and Information Systems Regulations 2018 (NIS Regulations): Implement the EU NIS Directive in UK law and require operators of essential services, including NHS trusts, health boards and other healthcare providers in the health sector, to take appropriate and proportionate technical and organisational measures to manage risks to the security of network and information systems and to minimise the impact of incidents. Competent authorities for the health sector are the Department of Health and Social Care for England, with the Information Commissioner as the competent authority for relevant digital service providers. Operators must notify their competent authority without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of an incident that has a significant impact on the continuity of the essential service. Penalties of up to GBP 17 million apply to the most serious failures. The NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT), which maps to the National Cyber Security Centre's Cyber Assessment Framework, is the means by which NHS organisations show compliance. The UK is considering new cyber security legislation that would update the regulations.

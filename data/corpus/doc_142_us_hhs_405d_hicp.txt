Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices (HICP), the HHS 405(d) programme's voluntary cybersecurity guidance for healthcare organisations, developed under s. 405(d) of the Cybersecurity Act of 2015: Identifies the five most common threats as email phishing attacks, ransomware attacks, loss or theft of equipment or data, insider, accidental or intentional data loss, and attacks against network-connected medical devices. Recommends ten cybersecurity practices: email protection systems; endpoint protection systems; access management; data protection and loss prevention; asset management; network management; vulnerability management; security operations centre and incident response; network connected medical devices; and cybersecurity oversight and governance. Separate technical volumes cover small, medium and large organisations. Under the HITECH Act amendment of 2021 (Public Law 116-321), HHS must consider whether a covered entity or business associate has had recognized security practices in place for the previous 12 months, such as HICP or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, when determining HIPAA penalties and audits.

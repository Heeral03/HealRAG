USCDI (United States Core Data for Interoperability): A standardised set of health data classes and data elements for nationwide, interoperable health information exchange, maintained by ASTP/ONC and expanded each year through a public process. Data classes include allergies and intolerances, assessment and plan of treatment, care team members, clinical notes, goals, health concerns, immunisations, laboratory, medications, patient demographics, problems, procedures, provenance, and vital signs. Version history: USCDI v1 is the baseline of the 2015 Edition certification and the Cures Act EHI definition; v2 (July 2021); v3 (July 2022) adds health insurance information, sexual orientation and gender identity and other items, and is required by HTI-1 from 1 January 2026; v4 (July 2023) adds clinical notes and facility data; v5 (July 2024) adds more elements. USCDI is implemented in FHIR through the US Core profiles. It is the US analogue of the European patient summary dataset in the EHDS.

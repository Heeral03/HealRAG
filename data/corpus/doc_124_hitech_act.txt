HITECH Act (Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act), enacted 17 February 2009 as Title XIII of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act: Promoted adoption of electronic health records through Medicare and Medicaid incentive payments (the Meaningful Use programme, later Promoting Interoperability). Strengthened HIPAA by: creating the first federal breach notification requirements for covered entities and business associates; making business associates directly subject to the Security Rule and parts of the Privacy Rule; prohibiting the sale of PHI without authorization and restricting marketing uses; giving individuals the right to an electronic copy of PHI held in an electronic health record and to restrict disclosures to a health plan for services paid in full out of pocket; and establishing tiered civil penalties and state attorney general enforcement. The 2013 HIPAA Omnibus Rule implemented most of these HITECH provisions in the HIPAA regulations. HITECH also created the HHS Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology (ONC) in statute, and in 2016 the 21st Century Cures Act added the information blocking prohibition.

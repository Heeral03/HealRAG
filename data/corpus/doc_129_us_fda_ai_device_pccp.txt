FDA regulation of AI in medical devices: Software that meets the definition of a device, including software as a medical device (SaMD), is regulated under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, and AI-enabled device software functions are typically cleared through 510(k), De Novo or premarket approval. In December 2024 FDA issued final guidance on Predetermined Change Control Plans (PCCPs) for AI-enabled device software functions. A PCCP, authorised under s. 515C of the FD&C Act, lets a manufacturer describe in a marketing submission the specific planned modifications to an AI model, the protocol for developing, validating and implementing them, and an impact assessment. If FDA authorises the PCCP, the manufacturer may implement the described changes without a new marketing submission. A PCCP has three components: a description of modifications, a modification protocol covering data management, retraining practices, performance evaluation and update procedures, and an impact assessment of benefits and risks. FDA also expects manufacturers to address transparency, bias and the representativeness of training data across patient populations, and cybersecurity under s. 524B of the FD&C Act.

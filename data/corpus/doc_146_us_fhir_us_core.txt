HL7 FHIR US Core Implementation Guide: The US national baseline of FHIR R4 profiles, extensions and search parameters for exchanging the US Core Data for Interoperability (USCDI), required by ONC certification and used by CMS and payer APIs. Key profiles include US Core Patient (identifier, name, gender, birth date, and the must-support extensions race, ethnicity and birth sex), AllergyIntolerance, Condition, Encounter, Immunization, MedicationRequest, Observation (with laboratory, vital signs, smoking status and social history variants), Procedure and DocumentReference. US Core 6.1.0 aligns with USCDI v3, the version required by HTI-1 from 1 January 2026; US Core 7.0.0 aligns with USCDI v4. US Core is the US counterpart of the UK Core profiles used in NHS England and the European EHDS patient summary profiles. US servers support the RESTful interactions read and search, and are authorised with SMART App Launch OAuth 2.0 scopes.

HHS OCR guidance on methods for de-identification of PHI under 45 CFR 164.514: Explains the two HIPAA methods. Under Safe Harbor, the eighteen listed identifiers must be removed and the entity must not know that the rest could identify an individual; the guidance clarifies that dates must be reduced to year and that ages over 89 must be aggregated into a 90 or older category. Under Expert Determination, the expert applies statistical or scientific principles to conclude that the risk of identification by an anticipated recipient is very small, and documents the methods and results. The guidance notes that there is no explicit numerical threshold for very small risk and that the expert may take into account replicability, data source availability and distinguishability. De-identified data can be released without HIPAA restrictions, but re-identification codes derived from patient information are not permitted. Data that is de-identified under HIPAA may still be personal data under the GDPR, which has a different anonymisation standard based on whether identification is reasonably likely by any means.

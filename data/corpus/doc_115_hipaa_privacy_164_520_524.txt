HIPAA Privacy Rule individual rights: Notice of Privacy Practices (45 CFR 164.520): most covered entities must give individuals a plain-language notice describing how PHI may be used and disclosed, the individual's rights, and the entity's legal duties, and must make a good-faith effort to obtain written acknowledgement of receipt from direct treatment providers. Right of access (45 CFR 164.524): individuals have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of PHI in a designated record set, including medical and billing records, and to direct that a copy be sent to a third party they designate. A covered entity must act on an access request within 30 days, with one 30-day extension if it gives the individual a written reason and a date. If the PHI is kept electronically, the individual may ask for an electronic copy in the form and format requested if readily producible. A covered entity may charge only a reasonable, cost-based fee covering labour for copying, supplies, postage and a summary if agreed, and may not charge for searching or retrieval. Access may be denied only for limited reasons, such as psychotherapy notes or information compiled for legal proceedings, and certain denials are reviewable.

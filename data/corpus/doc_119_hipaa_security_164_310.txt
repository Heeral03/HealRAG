HIPAA Security Rule physical safeguards, 45 CFR 164.310: Covered entities and business associates must implement physical measures, policies and procedures to protect electronic information systems and related buildings and equipment from natural and environmental hazards and unauthorised intrusion. Standards: facility access controls (contingency operations, facility security plan, access control and validation procedures, maintenance records); workstation use, which specifies the proper functions and physical surroundings of workstations that access ePHI; workstation security, restricting physical access to authorised users; and device and media controls, covering disposal and media re-use of hardware and electronic media that hold ePHI, accountability through records of hardware and media movements, and data backup and storage before equipment is moved.

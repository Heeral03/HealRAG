NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0, released 26 February 2024: A voluntary framework of outcomes for managing cybersecurity risk, applicable to organisations of all sizes and sectors, including healthcare. CSF 2.0 organises outcomes into six Functions: Govern (new in 2.0: cybersecurity risk management strategy, roles, policy and oversight), Identify (asset management, risk assessment, improvement), Protect (identity management and access control, awareness and training, data security, platform security, technology infrastructure resilience), Detect (continuous monitoring, adverse event analysis), Respond (incident management, analysis, reporting, communication and mitigation) and Recover (incident recovery plan execution and communication). It uses Profiles to describe current and target cybersecurity posture and Tiers to characterise rigour of risk management. Healthcare organisations commonly use the CSF to document recognised security practices alongside HIPAA Security Rule compliance and HHS 405(d) HICP.

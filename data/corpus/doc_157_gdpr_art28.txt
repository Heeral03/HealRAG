GDPR Article 28 - Processor: The controller may use only processors providing sufficient guarantees to implement appropriate technical and organisational measures. Processing by a processor must be governed by a contract or other legal act that is binding on the processor and sets out the subject-matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the type of personal data and categories of data subjects, and the obligations and rights of the controller. The contract must stipulate that the processor: processes personal data only on documented instructions from the controller; ensures persons authorised to process are committed to confidentiality; takes all measures required under Art. 32; respects conditions for engaging another processor (sub-processor) and remains fully liable to the controller for the sub-processor; assists the controller with data subject requests and with Articles 32 to 36; deletes or returns all personal data at the end of the services; and makes available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allows audits. This is the GDPR counterpart of the HIPAA business associate agreement.

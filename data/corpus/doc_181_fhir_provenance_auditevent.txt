FHIR resources for accountability: The Provenance resource records the entities and processes involved in producing, delivering or influencing a resource, including who (agent), what (entity), when (recorded) and why (reason), and may carry a digital signature. It supports tracing the origin and authorship of clinical data. The AuditEvent resource records security-relevant events, such as a user reading a patient record, a query, a login or a break-glass emergency override, with the type and subtype of the action, outcome, recorded time, the agent (user or system), the source system, and the entities (such as the patient) involved. AuditEvent records satisfy audit-control requirements such as the HIPAA Security Rule 45 CFR 164.312(b), GDPR accountability (Art. 5(2)) and Art. 32 expectations, the NHS DSPT, and the EHDS requirement to log access to electronic health data. Implementations commonly use the IHE ATNA profile with FHIR AuditEvent. Consent decisions can be linked with the Consent resource so that disclosures can be shown to match recorded patient preferences.

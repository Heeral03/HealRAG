NIS2 Directive, Directive (EU) 2022/2555, which Member States had to transpose by 17 October 2024: Sets cybersecurity risk management and incident reporting obligations for essential and important entities. The health sector is listed in Annex I (sectors of high criticality) and covers healthcare providers, EU reference laboratories, entities carrying out research and development of medicinal products, manufacturers of basic pharmaceutical products and preparations, and manufacturers of medical devices considered critical during a public health emergency. Art. 21 requires appropriate and proportionate technical, operational and organisational measures including risk analysis and information system security policies, incident handling, business continuity such as backup management and disaster recovery, supply chain security, vulnerability handling, cryptography and encryption, access control and multi-factor authentication. Art. 23 incident reporting: an early warning to the CSIRT or competent authority within 24 hours of becoming aware of a significant incident, an incident notification within 72 hours, and a final report within one month. Management bodies must approve and oversee the measures and can be held liable. Fines for essential entities reach at least EUR 10 million or 2 percent of worldwide annual turnover.

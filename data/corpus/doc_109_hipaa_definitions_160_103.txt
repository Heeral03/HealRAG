HIPAA Definitions, 45 CFR 160.103: A covered entity is (1) a health plan, (2) a health care clearinghouse, or (3) a health care provider who transmits any health information in electronic form in connection with a standard transaction covered by HIPAA. A business associate is a person or organisation that, on behalf of a covered entity, creates, receives, maintains or transmits protected health information for a regulated function or provides certain services (legal, actuarial, accounting, consulting, data aggregation, management, administrative, accreditation or financial) involving PHI. Subcontractors that handle PHI for a business associate are themselves business associates. Protected health information (PHI) is individually identifiable health information transmitted or maintained in electronic or any other form or medium, with limited exceptions such as education records and employment records held by a covered entity as employer. Individually identifiable health information relates to a person's past, present or future physical or mental health, the provision of health care to the person, or payment for that care, and identifies the person or could reasonably be used to identify them. Information that is de-identified under 45 CFR 164.514 is not PHI.

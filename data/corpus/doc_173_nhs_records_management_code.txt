NHS Records Management Code of Practice 2021: Sets minimum retention periods for health and care records in England, which apply to paper and electronic records. Adult health records, including GP records: 8 years after the last contact, or, for GP records of a deceased patient, 10 years after death. Children's records: until the child's 25th birthday, or until their 26th birthday if they were 17 at the conclusion of treatment. Mental health records, for patients under the Mental Health Act: 20 years after the last entry, or 8 years after death if the patient died while in care. Maternity records and some obstetric and cancer records: 25 years after the last contact. Records of people who lack capacity or who have a diagnosis of Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease have longer periods. At the end of the retention period, records must be reviewed and then either destroyed securely, with an auditable destruction log, or preserved if of archive value; records should not be kept longer than necessary, in line with UK GDPR Art. 5(1)(e) storage limitation.

FHIR Patient Resource Definition: The Patient resource covers data about patients. It represents demographic and administrative information about a person or animal receiving care or other health-related services. Key attributes include: identifier (unique identifiers like SSN), active (boolean to mark record state), name (HumanName structure for family and given names), telecom (contact details), gender (administrative gender values: male, female, other, unknown), birthDate (patient Date of Birth), and address (physical addresses).

EDPB Guidelines 03/2020 on the processing of data concerning health for the purpose of scientific research: Confirms that the GDPR and Art. 89 provide a specific regime for scientific research and that special category health data may be processed for research only if there is both an Art. 6 lawful basis and an Art. 9(2) condition. Relevant Art. 9(2) conditions are explicit consent (a), public interest in public health (i), and scientific research (j) based on Union or Member State law. Researchers must respect the data protection principles of Art. 5, with data minimisation, storage limitation and security, and implement the safeguards of Art. 89(1), such as pseudonymisation and, where research aims can be met, anonymisation. Transparency: data subjects must be informed, with limited exceptions where providing information would prove impossible or involve disproportionate effort. Data subject rights may be restricted for research under Art. 89(2) where national law so provides. International transfers for research follow Ch. V: adequacy, appropriate safeguards or narrowly interpreted derogations.

HIPAA Privacy Rule, 45 CFR 164.506 (uses and disclosures for treatment, payment and health care operations, often shortened to TPO): A covered entity may use and disclose PHI for its own treatment, payment or health care operations without the individual's authorization. Treatment means providing, coordinating or managing health care, including consultations and referrals between providers. Payment covers activities such as billing, claims management, determining eligibility and coverage, and utilisation review. Health care operations include quality assessment and improvement, competency review and training, accreditation, medical review, legal services, auditing, and business planning and management. A covered entity may also disclose PHI for another covered entity's treatment and payment activities, and for certain health care operations of the recipient if both have a relationship with the individual and the disclosure is for quality or fraud-related purposes. Consent for TPO is optional under HIPAA: a covered entity may choose to seek the individual's consent, but is not required to. Disclosures for TPO remain subject to the minimum necessary standard, except for treatment disclosures between providers.

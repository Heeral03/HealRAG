Common Rule, 45 CFR Part 46 Subpart A (Federal Policy for the Protection of Human Subjects): Applies to research involving human subjects conducted or supported by federal departments and agencies that have adopted it. Research must be reviewed by an Institutional Review Board (IRB), which approves it only if risks are minimised and reasonable in relation to anticipated benefits, subject selection is equitable, informed consent is sought and documented, and there are adequate provisions to protect privacy and confidentiality. The Revised Common Rule, effective 21 January 2019, requires informed consent to begin with a concise summary of key information, introduces broad consent for storage and secondary research use of identifiable private information and biospecimens, requires single IRB review for most multi-site studies, and adds exemptions for secondary research with identifiable information if the data are publicly available, recorded so subjects cannot be readily identified, or regulated by HIPAA as health care operations, research or public health. A research study may need both IRB review under the Common Rule and compliance with the HIPAA Privacy Rule, which requires an authorization or a waiver for research use of PHI.

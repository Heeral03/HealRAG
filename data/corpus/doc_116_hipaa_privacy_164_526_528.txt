HIPAA Privacy Rule individual rights, continued: Right to amend (45 CFR 164.526): an individual may ask a covered entity to amend PHI in a designated record set that is inaccurate or incomplete. The covered entity must act within 60 days, with one 30-day extension, and may deny the request if it did not create the information, if the information is not part of the designated record set, is not available for inspection, or is accurate and complete. If it denies, the individual may submit a statement of disagreement that becomes part of the record. Accounting of disclosures (45 CFR 164.528): an individual may request a list of certain disclosures of their PHI made in the six years before the request. The accounting excludes disclosures for treatment, payment and health care operations, disclosures to the individual, disclosures made under an authorization, and incidental disclosures. The covered entity must respond within 60 days. Other rights include the right to request restrictions (164.522(a)), including the right to restrict disclosures to a health plan when the individual pays out of pocket in full, and the right to confidential communications (164.522(b)).

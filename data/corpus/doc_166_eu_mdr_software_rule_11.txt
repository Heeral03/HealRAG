EU Medical Devices Regulation (MDR), Regulation (EU) 2017/745, Annex VIII Rule 11: Software intended to provide information which is used to take decisions with diagnosis or therapeutic purposes is classified as class IIa, except if such decisions have an impact that may cause death or an irreversible deterioration of a person's state of health, in which case it is class III, or a serious deterioration of health or a surgical intervention, in which case it is class IIb. Software intended to monitor physiological processes is class IIa, or class IIb if it monitors vital physiological parameters where variations could result in immediate danger to the patient. All other software is class I. Software classified above class I requires conformity assessment by a notified body, a quality management system, clinical evaluation, post-market surveillance, and registration in EUDAMED with a unique device identifier. The MDR applies from 26 May 2021. Medical device software that is also a high-risk AI system is subject to the AI Act requirements alongside the MDR.

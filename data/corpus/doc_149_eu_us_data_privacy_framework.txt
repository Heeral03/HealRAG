EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF), adopted by the European Commission on 10 July 2023 as Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 under GDPR Art. 45: Finds that the United States ensures an adequate level of protection for personal data transferred from the EU to US organisations that self-certify to the framework, so transfers to certified organisations need no further transfer tool such as standard contractual clauses. Eligible US organisations are those subject to the investigatory and enforcement powers of the Federal Trade Commission or the Department of Transportation. Certified organisations must follow the DPF Principles on notice, choice, accountability for onward transfer, security, data integrity and purpose limitation, access, and recourse, enforcement and liability, with special rules for sensitive data such as health data (opt-in consent for disclosure to a third party or use for a new purpose). The decision relies on US Executive Order 14086 of October 2022, which limits signals intelligence activities to what is necessary and proportionate and creates a Data Protection Review Court for redress. The decision is reviewed periodically and has been challenged before the EU courts. For EU health data going to a US recipient without DPF certification, GDPR Articles 46 and 49 transfer tools apply.

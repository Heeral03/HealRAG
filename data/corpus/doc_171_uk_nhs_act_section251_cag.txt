Section 251 of the NHS Act 2006 and the Health Service (Control of Patient Information) Regulations 2002: Allow confidential patient information to be processed without consent for medical purposes in England and Wales where it is not practicable to obtain consent and anonymised information cannot achieve the purpose, setting aside the common law duty of confidentiality. Medical purposes include medical research and the management of health and social care services. Regulation 2 covers communicable diseases and other risks to public health, regulation 3 covers cancer registries, and regulation 5 covers applications for support for other medical purposes. In England the Confidentiality Advisory Group (CAG) of the Health Research Authority advises on applications for research, and the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care makes decisions on non-research applications. Support is time-limited, reviewed annually, and conditional on safeguards, security, and the National Data Opt-out. s. 251 support is a legal gateway for the common law only; the processing still needs a UK GDPR lawful basis and Art. 9 condition. s. 251 support is not required where processing relies on consent, or where the data are anonymised.

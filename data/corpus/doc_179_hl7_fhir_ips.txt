HL7 International Patient Summary (IPS) FHIR Implementation Guide: Defines a minimal, specialty-agnostic, condition-independent dataset of essential health information for unscheduled, cross-border care, expressed as a FHIR Composition inside a Bundle of type document. Required sections include problems, allergies and intolerances, and medications; recommended sections include immunisations, results, procedures, medical devices, and advance directives. Profiles include Patient, Condition, AllergyIntolerance, MedicationStatement and MedicationRequest, Observation, Immunization and Procedure, using terminologies such as SNOMED CT for conditions and procedures, LOINC for observations and results, and ATC or national codes for medicines. The IPS is the basis for the European Patient Summary in MyHealth@EU and for the EHDS priority category 'patient summaries', and is used by UK and US initiatives, including the IPS-based Patient Summary specifications in HL7 Europe. It is mapped to CDA templates for legacy systems.

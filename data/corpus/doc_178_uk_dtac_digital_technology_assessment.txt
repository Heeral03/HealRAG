NHS Digital Technology Assessment Criteria (DTAC): A national baseline of five areas against which suppliers of digital health technologies, such as apps and software, must show assurance when selling to the NHS and adult social care. The five sections are: company information; clinical safety (including compliance with DCB0129 and a named Clinical Safety Officer); data protection (including a data protection impact assessment, a data processing role, UK GDPR compliance and the Data Security and Protection Toolkit); technical security (including Cyber Essentials Plus, penetration testing and secure development); and interoperability (including use of standards such as HL7 FHIR, SNOMED CT and NHS Number use). A usability and accessibility section requires a product accessible under WCAG 2.2 AA standards. Medical device software also needs UKCA or CE marking. The DTAC is a standard form completed by suppliers and reviewed by commissioners; it does not replace procurement due diligence.

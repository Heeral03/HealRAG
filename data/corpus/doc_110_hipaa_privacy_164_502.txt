HIPAA Privacy Rule, 45 CFR 164.502 (general rules): A covered entity or business associate may not use or disclose protected health information except as the Privacy Rule permits or requires. It must disclose PHI to the individual when they request access (164.524) and to HHS when it investigates compliance. It may use or disclose PHI for treatment, payment and health care operations (164.506), with a valid authorization (164.508), or for the public-interest purposes listed in 164.512. Minimum necessary standard (164.502(b)): when using or disclosing PHI or requesting it from another entity, a covered entity must make reasonable efforts to limit PHI to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose. The minimum necessary standard does not apply to disclosures to a health care provider for treatment, to the individual, to disclosures made under an authorization, to disclosures to HHS, or to uses and disclosures required by law. Psychotherapy notes receive stronger protection and generally require specific authorization. A covered entity may disclose PHI to a business associate only under a written business associate contract (164.502(e), 164.504(e)).

HIPAA Privacy Rule, 45 CFR 164.508 (authorization): A covered entity must obtain a valid written authorization for any use or disclosure of PHI that the Privacy Rule does not otherwise permit, including most marketing, any sale of PHI, and uses and disclosures of psychotherapy notes. A valid authorization must be in plain language and contain: a specific and meaningful description of the information; who may disclose it; who may receive it; the purpose; an expiration date or event; the individual's signature and date; a statement of the right to revoke in writing; a statement that treatment, payment, enrolment or eligibility generally cannot be conditioned on signing; and a statement that information disclosed may be redisclosed and no longer protected by HIPAA. An individual may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent the covered entity has already acted in reliance on it. Marketing authorizations must disclose any financial remuneration. A sale of PHI requires an authorization stating that the disclosure will result in remuneration. A covered entity must give the individual a copy of the signed authorization and keep it for six years.

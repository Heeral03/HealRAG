TEFCA (Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement): A nationwide framework for health information exchange established by the 21st Century Cures Act, run by ONC (now ASTP/ONC) with the Recognized Coordinating Entity (RCE), The Sequoia Project. Qualified Health Information Networks (QHINs) are designated networks that connect to each other under the Common Agreement; participants and subparticipants such as providers, payers, public health agencies and individual access services connect through QHINs. Permitted exchange purposes are: treatment, payment, health care operations, public health, government benefits determination, and individual access services. Common Agreement version 2.0, released in 2024, adds support for FHIR-based exchange. TEFCA participants that are HIPAA covered entities or business associates remain subject to HIPAA; non-HIPAA participants must agree to comply with HIPAA-like privacy and security requirements and breach notification. Comparable cross-border arrangement in the EU: MyHealth@EU under the European Health Data Space.

HIPAA business associate agreements (BAAs), 45 CFR 164.502(e), 164.504(e) and 164.308(b): A covered entity may let a business associate create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI on its behalf only if it obtains satisfactory assurances, in a written contract, that the business associate will appropriately safeguard the information. A BAA must: establish the permitted and required uses and disclosures; prohibit uses or disclosures that would violate the Privacy Rule if done by the covered entity; require appropriate safeguards and compliance with the Security Rule's requirements for electronic PHI; require reporting of unauthorised uses and disclosures, including breaches of unsecured PHI and security incidents; require the business associate to bind its subcontractors to the same restrictions; make PHI available to support the individual's rights of access, amendment and accounting; make books and records available to HHS; and require return or destruction of PHI at termination if feasible. Since the 2013 Omnibus Rule and the HITECH Act, business associates are directly liable for Security Rule compliance, for impermissible uses and disclosures, for failing to provide breach notification to the covered entity, and for failing to enter BAAs with their subcontractors.

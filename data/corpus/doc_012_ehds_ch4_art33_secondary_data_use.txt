EHDS Chapter IV Article 33 - Categories of electronic health data for secondary use: Data holders shall make available for secondary use the following categories of electronic health data: (a) EHRs; (b) data factors influencing health, including socio-economic, environmental and lifestyle factors; (c) genetic, genomic and proteomic data; (d) person-generated electronic health data; (e) data from clinical trials; (f) register data of medicinal products and devices.

EU Data Act, Regulation (EU) 2023/2854, applicable from 12 September 2025: Gives users of connected products and related services, including connected medical and wellness devices, the right to access and share the data generated by their use, and obliges data holders to make such data available to the user and to third parties chosen by the user on fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory terms. Personal data in such datasets remain subject to the GDPR, and the Data Act does not create a legal basis for processing it. It also contains rules on switching between data processing (cloud) services and on unfair contractual terms in data sharing. Data Governance Act, Regulation (EU) 2022/868, applicable since 24 September 2023: sets conditions for re-use of protected public-sector data (including health data), regulates data intermediation services, and creates a framework for data altruism organisations that collect data made available voluntarily by individuals or companies for general-interest purposes, such as scientific research. The European Health Data Space builds on both instruments for secondary use of health data.

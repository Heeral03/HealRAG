CMS interoperability rules: The CMS Interoperability and Patient Access final rule (CMS-9115-F, 2020) requires CMS-regulated payers, including Medicare Advantage organisations, Medicaid and CHIP agencies and managed care plans, and qualified health plan issuers on federally facilitated exchanges, to provide a FHIR R4 Patient Access API that gives members their claims, encounter and clinical data, using the HL7 FHIR US Core profiles and SMART on FHIR authorisation. The Interoperability and Prior Authorization final rule (CMS-0057-F), published February 2024, extends this: payers must implement Provider Access, Payer-to-Payer and Prior Authorization APIs built on FHIR; must send prior authorization decisions within 72 hours for expedited requests and seven calendar days for standard requests starting in 2026; must give specific reasons for denials; and must publicly report prior authorization metrics. The API requirements apply from 1 January 2027. The rules complement HIPAA's right of access and ONC's information blocking rules.
